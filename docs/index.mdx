Open **My account** from Console for password, passkey and multi-factor settings. Existing authentication, OAuth and Studio MCP protocol addresses remain compatibility contracts; use the endpoints in the [authentication guide](/developers/authentication).

## Understand your workspace

A **workspace** groups members and resources. A **project** organizes applications and services within that workspace. Website access additionally depends on its CMS binding and your website permissions. Being listed as a customer contact does not grant access.

[Accounts and access](/guides/access) explains membership and security. [Plans and limits](/guides/plans-and-billing) explains why an available product may still have no remaining capacity.

## Connect and deploy with clear expectations

New PostgreSQL work starts with [your own Neon connection](/guides/byok-neon). Existing managed databases remain available for maintenance. A provider connection establishes authorization; it does not automatically create or deploy resources.

Git builds, incoming Mail and plugin activation have their own availability checks. A successful image build is separate from a healthy deployment. A published plugin image is separate from a signed registry release and an installed plugin. Follow the relevant guide and the state shown for your workspace.

<Note>
Automatically assigned addresses for enabled public applications and websites use `<project>.spitzli.site`. They are assigned by the platform after admission; an arbitrary subdomain is not automatically active. Existing customer domains remain valid.
</Note>

If a workspace, connection or resource is missing, start with [troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting).
