---
title: "Accounts and access"
description: "Sign in, secure your account and check workspace and website access."
---

Open [Console](https://console.spitzli.cloud) with the email address approved for your account. Account identity, workspace membership and website permissions are separate. A customer's contact email does not grant access.

## First sign-in

If you have not set your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Enter your confirmed account email, open the single-use email link and choose your password. Complete any multi-factor challenge shown after sign-in.

Spitzli Cloud uses Managed Neon Auth for sign-in. Core retains account approval, workspace roles, website grants, MFA and passkey checks. Connecting your own Neon account is a [separate provider connection](/guides/byok-neon).

## Workspace membership

An authorized administrator invites members. Complete account setup and accept the invitation before opening the workspace. Owners, administrators and members have different permissions; workspace roles do not grant platform operator access. Last-administrator protections can block removal.

For websites, owners and administrators of a mapped workspace can receive administrator access. Members need an enabled website grant. A revoked grant, disabled binding or suspended account can still prevent access.

## Secure your account

Open **Account security** from Console's account menu to manage your password, passkeys, MFA and recovery codes. Operators must complete the required security setup. MFA-enabled accounts must complete the additional challenge after their password is accepted.

<Note>Existing passkeys and authentication issuer addresses remain bound to `auth.webdock.dev`. Start account actions from Console; changing the hostname of an authentication link can break the flow.</Note>

Central sign-out invalidates access tied to the current session. Manage other sessions separately. Website CMS access uses your Spitzli Cloud identity rather than an independent website password.
